# 📊 Retail Sales Analytics

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end retail sales analytics workflow using Excel, Power Query, MySQL, SQL, and Power BI.

The objective was to transform raw relational business data into clean, validated, analysis-ready data and use it to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, products, returns, inventory, and business trends.

## 📂 Dataset

The project works with 9 related business tables covering customers, orders, products, sales, returns, inventory, warehouses, sales representatives, and monthly targets.

Key dataset volumes:

- 25,000 Customers
- 120,000 Orders
- 303,254 Order Lines
- 600 Products
- 60 Sales Representatives
- 9 Warehouses
- 15,812+ Return Records
- 4,840 Inventory Records

## 💡 Key Findings

- Delivered orders generated approximately **₹41.67 Cr in net sales**.
- The business generated approximately **₹13.32 Cr gross margin**, representing a **31.98% gross margin rate**.
- **110,291 delivered orders** accounted for **453,192 units sold**.
- Average Order Value was approximately **₹3,778**.
- October 2025 recorded a **29.45% Month-over-Month increase** in net sales.
- January 2026 recorded an **18.70% Month-over-Month decline**.
- Sales recovered by **14.04% in March 2026**.
- Sales remained comparatively stable from May through August 2026.
